# Bench Specifications

The bench beside a reconstruction, and the things put on it to be worked on.
Implemented in `crates/sfmtool-core/src/bench/`, bound as
`sfmtool.bench`.

| Document | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| [bench.md](bench.md) | The bench value: the ordered list of labelled items, where a label is minted from and how a collision is suffixed, and why every operation returns a new bench. |
| [editable-track.md](editable-track.md) | The editable track: its observations with their provenance, per-stage measurements and verdicts, the two representations it passes through, the evaluation that measures it at either and the transitions between them, the descriptor search that grows one from an index of the capture, and the steps that grow, judge, split and finally commit one as a point. |
| [refit-points.md](refit-points.md) | Refitting every point: each point put through the bench's fit and the commit's record with every observation kept, held points left out, ranged points read at their distance, points the fit refuses kept as they were, every answer written at its own row of one new reconstruction, and the measured run time. |
| [grown-plane-normal.md](grown-plane-normal.md) | The grown-plane normal: one plane shared by every view fitted directly to the photographs, each picked view's window grown from its keypoint to the whole patch by Levenberg–Marquardt on a robust cell score, the views picked near to far by predicted information and their planes combined by a robust information-weighted mean with the widest pairs, the weak-turn rule, the precision it reports, and the shifts it reports without writing a keypoint. |
| [grown-plane-normal-measurements.md](grown-plane-normal-measurements.md) | The measurements behind the grown-plane normal's design and defaults on the three ground truths: the keypoint start and no search, the agreement rule, the selection and the near-to-far ladder, the weak-turn rule judged by eye, the optimiser's Sobel gradient and stops, the cheaper growths, the Sobel information and σ target, each view's slid patch, and writing no keypoint. |
| [refine-normals.md](refine-normals.md) | Refining every point's normal: each finite point put through the bench's grown-plane normal step and the commit's record with every observation kept, its centre unchanged, held and ranged points refined with their constraint kept, points at infinity skipped and counted, points the step leaves unchanged or refuses kept as they were, the refit's writer, and the measured run time. |
| [track-at-pixel.md](track-at-pixel.md) | Building a track at a pixel: the cascade of four ways of finding the other photographs' sightings (the `.matches` clusters, the neighbours' affine transfer, a plane sweep and the SIFT index's constellation query), the shared finish that anchors the patch on the pixel, grows, cleans and gates it, and the report and refusal a query returns. |
| [far-field-sweep.md](far-field-sweep.md) | The far-field sweep: a pixel's patch read from infinity inwards along its ray in every image it lands in, each peak of that reading returned with its range and metrics, its images grouped pairwise by their middles and refitted when the queried image stands apart; and the patch read along a ray and the grey images it samples, which the sweep is built from. |
| [distance-range.md](distance-range.md) | The distance range a point's sightings allow along a pixel's ray, found by stepping out from the triangulated distance and bisecting where a sighting leaves its tolerance; the camera spread; and whether a range is bounded, far or neither. |
| [nearby-sources.md](nearby-sources.md) | The matching sources that propose candidate tracks near a pixel, each from its own evidence, and the one shape they all return: a point, its sightings with the queried image's first, and how well they meet. |
| [depth-layers.md](depth-layers.md) | The depth layers: the candidates near a pixel grouped by overlapping distance ranges, nearest first, each candidate's support from readings on other photographs, each layer read with the pixel's own patch, and its score, key, rank and confidence, with where the constants came from. |
| [nearby-tracks.md](nearby-tracks.md) | Finding the nearby tracks: the matching sources in order with the stopping rule, each candidate's range and class, the far-field sweep when the sources leave the pixel's distance open, the depth layers, the track-stage tracks the bench takes for them, and their labels by layer rank and distance from the pixel. |
| [nearby-tracks-measurements.md](nearby-tracks-measurements.md) | The measurements behind finding the nearby tracks: parity with the track-at-pixel harness's Python loop, the cost of finding against building the bench tracks, and how often the built tracks repeat one another. |
